No More CMY

Design in black, white and grey.

Submissions open periodically through open calls.
There is no open call at present. Guidelines and submission details will be published here when the next call opens.
No More CMY is an emerging collection of work in black, white and grey, giving these works a stage of their own.


Bringing together publications, graphic works and other visual practices, it offers a place to discover, revisit and draw inspiration from the collection.


A shared space for those who create, collect and appreciate it.
